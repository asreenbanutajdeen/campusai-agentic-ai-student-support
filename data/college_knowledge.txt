COLLEGE REGULATIONS
Minimum attendance requirement is 75 percent. Students with attendance below the required level may be restricted according to institutional regulations. Students should contact the department office for official permission or condonation procedures.
---
EXAMINATION
Internal assessment tests and semester examinations are conducted according to the academic calendar. Students should check official department notices for exact dates, rooms and instructions. Hall tickets must be carried for university examinations.
---
BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE
Students can request a bonafide certificate through the department/college office. The student should provide the required application details and collect the certificate after office processing.
---
TRANSFER CERTIFICATE
A transfer certificate is issued by the college office after completion of the required clearance and documentation process. Students should contact the office for the current application procedure.
---
SYLLABUS
The Information Technology curriculum includes programming, database management, computer networks, software engineering, artificial intelligence and machine learning, cloud computing and project work. The exact syllabus should be verified from the official university document.
---
FEES
Students should verify tuition, examination and other applicable fees from the official college accounts/office notice because fee amounts and deadlines can change.
---
LEAVE
Students should inform the concerned faculty/department when requesting leave and follow the institution's attendance and leave procedures.
---
PLACEMENT
Placement preparation may include aptitude, coding, communication, technical interviews and resume preparation. Students should follow placement-cell announcements for company-specific eligibility and schedules.
---
PROJECT
Final-year students are expected to complete an academic project under faculty guidance. Project reviews, documentation and demonstrations should follow the department schedule.
---
FAQ
For official academic decisions, students should rely on the latest college/university circulars and notices rather than chatbot output alone.
